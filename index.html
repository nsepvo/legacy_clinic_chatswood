Our approach

Root-cause medicine, delivered as everyday general practice.

That means careful history taking, appropriate investigation, and treatment plans built around metabolic health, hormones, chronic disease and prevention rather than symptoms alone.

Integrative

Conventional general practice alongside evidence-informed complementary care, coordinated in one place.

Functional

Looking for what is driving your symptoms, with thorough assessment and appropriate investigation before treatment.

Lifestyle

Practical, sustained work on sleep, nutrition, movement and stress as a genuine part of your treatment.

Common questions

Good to know before your visit

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. We're welcoming new patients of all ages. You can book online through HotDoc or call reception on (02) 8389 0444.

Do you bulk bill?

We bulk bill concession card holders and Medicare patients under 16. Other consultations are privately billed; see our fees above.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your Medicare card, any concession or DVA card, a list of your current medications, and any recent results, referrals or letters from other doctors. If you'd like your records sent from your previous clinic, ask them to forward them to reception@legacyclinic.com.au.

Can I get my results over the phone?

Please book a follow-up appointment to go through your results with your doctor. You're welcome to call and check whether they're back, but results aren't given over the phone unless your doctor has arranged it.

Do I need an appointment for repeat scripts or referrals?

Yes. Repeat prescriptions and referrals need a consultation, so please check what you need before your appointment. Referrals can't be backdated.

Is there parking?

We don't have dedicated parking, but paid street parking is available nearby. The clinic is a short walk from Chatswood Station and is wheelchair accessible, with both lift and stair access.
